1. Acceptance of Terms

2. Account & Security
  • Username must be at least 3 characters long and unique across the platform.
  • Password must be at least 6 characters. A combination of uppercase, lowercase, numbers and symbols is recommended.
  • You are solely responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your credentials.
  • Each account is entitled to one MetroCard and one digital wallet.
3. Ticketing & Booking Rules
  • Each ticket is valid only for the selected date, route, and number of passengers.
  • A maximum of 6 passengers can be booked per ticket.
  • QR codes are generated upon successful booking and must be scanned at entry and exit gates.
  • Tickets can be downloaded as PDF for offline access.
  • Travel date must be today or a future date; past-date bookings are not allowed.
  • Source and destination stations must be different.
4. Fare Calculation & Peak Hours

Fares are dynamically calculated based on station-to-station distance:

Component Details
Base Fare ₹10 (flat)
Per Kilometre ₹5/km
Minimum Fare ₹10
Peak Hour Surge +25% surcharge applied

Peak Hours: 8:00 AM – 11:00 AM & 5:00 PM – 7:00 PM. A 25% surge is automatically applied to the base fare during these hours.

5. Cancellation & Refund Policy

Tickets may be cancelled before the travel date. Refunds are credited to your MetroFlow wallet:

Cancellation Window Refund Status
More than 24 hours before travel 80% of fare Best Rate
Less than 24 hours before travel 50% of fare Partial
After travel date / Used ticket 0% — No refund Non-refundable
6. Digital Wallet
  • Every user receives a digital wallet upon registration with an initial balance of ₹0.
  • Wallet can be recharged with any positive amount.
  • Ticket fares are automatically deducted from the wallet at the time of booking.
  • Insufficient wallet balance will prevent ticket booking — please recharge beforehand.
  • All wallet transactions (recharges, deductions, refunds) are recorded in your transaction history.
7. MetroCard
  • Each user can issue one virtual MetroCard linked to their account.
  • MetroCard tracks your total trips and usage statistics.
  • Auto-recharge alert is triggered when the wallet balance falls below ₹50.
  • MetroCard cannot be transferred to another user.
8. Green Loyalty Points
  • Earn 1 Green Point for every ₹2 spent on ticket bookings.
  • Points are automatically credited upon successful booking.
  • Points balance is visible on your dashboard.

Example: A ₹100 ticket earns you 50 Green Points automatically.

9. Privacy & Data Protection
  • Passwords are securely hashed using SHA-256 encryption before storage.
  • Session-based authentication is used to protect your account.
  • Personal data is stored in a secure MySQL database and is never shared with third parties.
  • You may change your password at any time from the dashboard settings.
